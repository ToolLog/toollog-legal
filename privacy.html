Privacy Policy

ToolLog mobile application

Last updated: 7 August 2026

ToolLog (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the ToolLog mobile application (“App”) handles information. By downloading, installing, or using the App, you acknowledge this Policy.

1. Summary

ToolLog is designed for tradies and businesses to record tools, warranties, and insurance details. Your tool inventory is stored locally on your device using Apple’s on-device storage (SwiftData / local files). We do not operate a cloud account, user login system, or remote database for your tool inventory.

2. Information you provide

You may enter information into the App, including:

  • Your name or business name (optional, in Settings)
  • Tool details (names, manufacturers, models, serial numbers, values, categories, notes)
  • Photos of tools and receipts
  • Insurance / warranty details (policy names, numbers, limits, expiry dates)
  • Lending, service, transfer, and status notes
  • Contact or other details you choose to include in exported reports

This information is stored on your device. We do not receive a copy of your inventory unless you voluntarily send it to us (for example by emailing support).

3. Photos and files

Tool and receipt photos are saved on your device. They are included in PDF or other exports only when you choose to create and share those exports. We do not upload your photos to our servers.

4. Local notifications

The App can schedule reminders on your device (for example warranty expiry or tool return dates). Notification permission is optional and controlled by iOS.

5. Biometric app lock

If you enable App Lock, Face ID or Touch ID is used on your device to unlock the App. Biometric data is processed by Apple’s operating system and is not collected, stored, or transmitted by us.

6. Limited network use

The App may connect to the internet only for limited purposes such as:

  • Checking for updated depreciation rule files (optional / when available)
  • Opening manufacturer warranty or product web pages you choose to visit
  • Barcode or product lookup when scanning (if used)

We do not upload your tool inventory to our servers as part of normal App operation. Third-party websites you open are governed by their own privacy policies.

7. Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The App does not use third-party advertising SDKs and does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites for advertising. We do not sell your personal information.

8. Data retention and deletion

Your data remains on your device until you delete tools, use “Delete all data” in Settings, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App typically removes local App data. Device backups (for example iCloud or computer backups) may retain copies according to your Apple / device settings; those backups are outside our control.

9. Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information only if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights, or if you voluntarily provide information to us (for example support emails).

10. Security

Because inventory data is stored on your device, you are responsible for device security, passcodes, biometric lock, OS updates, and physical control of the device. No method of electronic storage is completely secure. We do not warrant that your data cannot be accessed if your device is lost, stolen, compromised, or backed up insecurely.

11. Australian privacy

Where the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) applies, we handle personal information in accordance with that Act. You may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information we hold (typically limited to support correspondence you send us). Inventory data on your device is controlled by you.

12. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Do not use the App to store children’s personal data.

13. International users

The App is designed primarily for use in Australia. If you use the App outside Australia, you do so at your own initiative and are responsible for compliance with local laws.

14. Changes

We may update this Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated Policy. Material changes may also be noted in an App update description where practical.
